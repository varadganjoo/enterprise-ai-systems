# PTO policy 2024

Status: superseded on 1 January 2026 by the 2026 PTO policy.

## Allowance

Full-time employees accrued 15 days of paid time off per calendar year under this 2024 policy.

## Rollover

The 2024 policy allowed no rollover. Unused days expired on 31 December.
