# PTO policy 2026

Status: current. This policy supersedes the 2024 PTO policy.

## Allowance

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off per calendar year under the current 2026 policy.

## Rollover

Up to 5 unused days may roll into the next calendar year. Days beyond 5 expire on 31 December.
